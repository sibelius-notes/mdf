# UWaterloo Course Subject Code Changes

Some course subject codes at the University of Waterloo have been renamed over the years. Notes on this site may use either the old or new code depending on when the course was taken.

| Old Code | New Code | Old Name | New Name | Notes |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|-------|
| SMF | SRF | Sexuality, Marriage & Family | Sexuality, Relationships & Family | Department renamed |
| MGMT | MSCI | Management Sciences | Management Science & Engineering | Department rebranded under Engineering |
| RS | RCS | Religious Studies | Religion, Culture & Spirituality | Program renamed in Faculty of Arts |
